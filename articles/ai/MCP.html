MCP: Beyond APIs — Building the Capability Layer for AI MCP is everywhere. But are we thinking about it the right way? MCP is no longer just a buzzword. It is becoming an important part of the conversation around AI agents and enterprise architecture. But after exploring MCP from the protocol, integration, security, tool-design and enterprise architecture perspectives, one question keeps coming back to me: Are we trying to expose more APIs to AI — or are we designing the right business capabilities for AI to safely use? I put together this 10-slide visual series to share my perspective — without turning it into another 20-page technical article. 🙂 The series covers: 🔹 What MCP really is — and what it isn't 🔹 MCP vs. APIs 🔹 Host, Client & Server architecture 🔹 Tools, Resources & Prompts 🔹 MCP in enterprise and SAP scenarios 🔹 Why more tools ≠ better AI agents 🔹 Security, identity, authorization & audit 🔹 Performance, scalability & cost 🔹 MCP + existing APIs and integration architecture 🔹 What I believe matters most for Enterprise AI One principle stands out for me: MCP standardizes the connection. Architecture determines the outcome. Security determines the trust. Business capabilities determine the value.
